Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · 日本語

Miyajin Product ("we", "us") sets out below how information is handled in the iOS app "What I Did" (Japanese name: 今日何した？) (the "App").

The App has no server of ours and no account registration. The records, categories, memos, plans and budgets you enter are stored only on your device, and we never receive them. Sleep data from Health and calendar events read for "Auto Drafts" are also handled only on your device and are never sent off it (Section 5).

1. Information handled

  1. Records: the time records you enter (start and end date and time, category, and a one-line memo).
  2. Categories and routines: category names, colors, icons, order and visibility, and templates for things you log often.
  3. Plans and budgets: plan templates for each weekday, plans for a single day, and weekly time budgets.
  4. Sleep from Health and calendar events: only if you turn on "Auto Drafts" and allow access in the iOS permission screen, the App reads sleep data from Apple Health and your calendar events (title and start and end date and time) (Section 5).
  5. Settings: notification settings, chart and clock styles, widget color, share image background, the expiry times of features unlocked by watching ads, and similar settings.
  6. Purchase status: whether you have purchased Premium. Purchases and payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information.
  7. Advertising identifiers, etc.: to deliver ads, ad providers may collect the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) and information about your device and usage (Section 6).

The App does not ask for or collect your name, email address or location.

2. Purposes of use

The information is used to (a) save, show and total your records (pie charts, stats and reviews), (b) show the Home Screen widget, (c) schedule reminder notifications, (d) provide Premium and ad-unlocked features, (e) fix problems, (f) deliver, optimize and measure ads, (g) compare your plans with your records and show time budgets, and (h) show draft entries made from Health and your calendar. Information read from Health and your calendar is used only for (h) and never for ads under (f) or for marketing.

3. Where data is stored

InformationStored in

Nothing is sent to or stored on our servers. The App does not sync with iCloud, but if device backups (such as iCloud Backup) are turned on, data on your device may be included in those backups.

4. Information sent outside the device

  1. Google AdMob: advertising identifiers and similar information may be sent to deliver ads (Section 6). Your records, categories, memos, plans, budgets and information read from Health and your calendar are never sent to ad providers.
  2. Apple (App Store): the App communicates with Apple to purchase and restore Premium.
  3. Sharing and exporting: chart images you share and records you export (CSV files) are passed only to the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet. We never receive them. How the destination handles them is governed by that service's terms.

Notifications are scheduled on your device and are not sent anywhere. We do not sell or provide your information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Health and calendar information (Auto Drafts)

  1. Only if you turn on "Auto Drafts" in "Account" → "Auto Drafts" in the App and allow access in the iOS permission screen, the App reads sleep data from Apple Health (HealthKit) and your calendar events. It is off by default, and you can use the rest of the App without allowing access.
  2. The information read is used on your device only to show draft entries on screen. It is never sent off your device, including to our servers.
  3. The information read is never used for advertising, marketing or other use-based data mining, and is never provided or sold to ad providers (including Google AdMob) or any other third party.
  4. The sleep data and events themselves are not stored. Only when you tap a draft to confirm it is its time and category (and, for an event, the event title as the memo) saved as an entry in the App on your device. In addition, only markers (identifiers) of drafts you confirmed or dismissed are stored on your device so the same draft is not shown again. Confirmed entries can be edited or deleted in the App at any time, like other entries. The App does not sync with iCloud.
  5. The App never writes to Health or your calendar.
  6. You can withdraw access at any time in the iOS Settings app under "Privacy & Security" → "Health" or "Calendars" (for Health, also from your profile in the Health app → "Apps"). Turning off the switch in "Auto Drafts" stops the App from reading.

6. Advertising and tracking (ATT)

  1. The App shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of some screens and, only when you choose "Watch Ad", a rewarded ad such as a video. Watching a rewarded ad lets you use the related feature for 24 hours.
  2. If you allow tracking in iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT), the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used for personalized ads. If you do not allow it, non-personalized ads are shown and you can still use the App.
  3. SKAdNetwork and similar mechanisms may be used to measure ad performance.
  4. In the EEA, the UK and other applicable regions, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown, and no ads are requested until consent is obtained. You can change your choice at any time from "Account" → "Ad Privacy Settings" in the App.
  5. If you purchase Premium, no ads are shown and the ad components are not started.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ad delivery) / Google User Messaging Platform (consent management)
  • Apple (App Store, In-App Purchase)
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) and Calendar (EventKit): iOS frameworks for reading information on your device. No information is sent to us or to third parties through them (Section 5).

Please see each company's privacy policy for how they handle information (Google Privacy Policy / Google advertising / Apple Privacy Policy).

8. Retention and deletion

  1. Deleting records: you can edit or delete records in the App at any time.
  2. Deleting the App: data on your device is erased when you delete the App (including its Home Screen widget). We cannot restore erased data.
  3. Health and calendar information: because the App does not store the information it reads, drafts stop appearing when you withdraw access or turn the switch off. Confirmed entries can be deleted in the App.
  4. Exported files: exported CSV files and shared images remain at the destination you chose. Delete them there if needed.

9. Your rights

Depending on the laws where you live (such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information. Because we keep no user information on servers, information on your device can be erased by editing or deleting it in the App or by deleting the App. For information collected by ad providers, use the consent settings above and see each provider's guidance. We do not sell personal information. If you have any questions, please contact us (Section 14).

10. International transfers

Through ad providers and similar services, information may be processed outside Japan.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to prevent the leakage, loss or damage of the information we handle.

12. Children's privacy

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

13. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy as needed. We will announce important changes in the App or by other means.

14. Contact
